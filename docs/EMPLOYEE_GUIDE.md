# Shop Staff & Worker Operational Manual

This manual provides clear instructions for daily shop floor staff, sales representatives, carpenters, and warehouse workers using **FurnitureOS**.

---

## 1. How to Find Products & Check Stock

1. Open **Products** or use the global keyboard search bar (`Ctrl + K` or top search box).
2. Type product name, SKU, or category.
3. Check the **Available Stock** counter before committing a sale to a customer.

---

## 2. How to Create a Quick Sale

1. Click **Sales** -> **New Sale**.
2. Type customer phone number or name. If new, click **+ Quick Add Customer**.
3. Click products to add them to the bill.
4. Select Payment Method (*Cash, UPI, Card, Bank Transfer*).
5. Click **Confirm & Print Invoice**.

---

## 3. How to Check In Worker Attendance

1. Go to **Workers** -> **Attendance**.
2. Find your name or employee code.
3. Mark status as **Present**, **Half Day**, or **Leave** with check-in time.

---

## 4. How to Update Production Work Orders

1. Open **Work Orders** -> Select your assigned task (e.g. *Cutting, Assembly, Sanding, Polishing*).
2. Click **Start Task** when beginning work.
3. Click **Mark Completed** upon finishing for Quality Check inspection.
