# Company Owner Setup & Operations Guide

Welcome to **FurnitureOS**! This guide walks furniture shop owners and business directors through setting up your business tenant, adding inventory, managing staff, generating sales, and reviewing financial reports.

---

## 1. Initial Company Setup

1. **Company Profile**: Go to **Settings** -> **Company Profile**.
   - Enter Company Name, Address, Phone, GST Number, Tax ID, and upload your Company Logo.
   - Configure stock rules (e.g. `Allow Negative Stock`).
2. **Payment Accounts**: Go to **Finance** -> **Payment Accounts**.
   - Set up your Cash Drawer, Bank Accounts, and UPI merchant handles with opening balances.

---

## 2. Staff & User Onboarding

1. Navigate to **Company** -> **Team Members**.
2. Click **Invite Member**.
3. Select Role:
   - **Manager**: Full access to sales, purchases, inventory, workers, and reports.
   - **Staff**: Access to POS Sales, Inventory search, and Customer creation.
   - **Worker**: Production task updates and attendance log access.

---

## 3. Product Catalog & Opening Inventory

1. **Categories & Units**: Set up furniture categories (e.g. *Sofas, Dining Tables, Office Chairs, Raw Timber, Fabrics*) and measurement units (*Pcs, Sets, Meters, Kg*).
2. **Add Products**: Go to **Products** -> **Add Product**.
   - Enter SKU, Name, Type (*Finished Product* vs *Raw Material*), Purchase Cost, Selling Price, Minimum Stock level, and Opening Stock.
   - Upload product photos directly via Cloudinary image uploader.

---

## 4. Sales POS & Invoicing

1. Go to **Sales** -> **New Sale**.
2. Select Customer (or create a quick walk-in customer).
3. Search and select items. Adjust quantity, discounts, or tax rates.
4. Click **Complete Sale**:
   - Stock quantities automatically decrease.
   - Customer payment is recorded to your selected cash/bank account.
   - Click **Print Invoice** or **Export PDF** to hand to the customer.

---

## 5. Purchase & Supplier Orders

1. Go to **Purchases** -> **New Purchase Order**.
2. Select Supplier and add raw materials or finished furniture items.
3. Mark as **Received** when stock arrives at your warehouse to automatically increase inventory levels.

---

## 6. Business Reports & Analytics

- Go to **Reports** to view Profit & Loss, Sales by Product, Inventory Valuation, GST Tax Reports, and Worker Payroll Summaries.
- Click **Export Excel** or **Export PDF** for tax filing or record keeping.
